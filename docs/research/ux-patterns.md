# UX patterns for the internal business app

Research date: 24 October 2026 · Scope: interaction and layout patterns only (CSS and technology choices are covered by a separate research file) · Status: recommendations for the UI work package (spec §6)

**Fixed requirements this research works within (from the product owner, not up for debate):** responsive; light mode only; mobile-first design system; **no horizontal scrolling anywhere**, including data lists and line-item grids; **complex forms are wizards with a clear progress bar**; server-rendered with templ + htmx, minimal Alpine.js, no SPA.

**Who uses it (from `docs/tests/core/00-fixtures.md`):** purchase staff (Priya, Ravi) mostly on desktop, raising POs every day; stores staff (Suresh) receiving goods on site on phones; every employee (for example Deepak) filing expense claims from a phone with receipt photos; approvers (Ravi, Meena) who will often decide from a phone; an administrator and a masters manager (Anil, Kavya) on desktop. INR, GST, lakh/crore grouping, `dd-mm-yyyy` dates.

**How to read the citations.** Every finding names its source and, where the source shows one, its publication date. "Code:" citations point at the read-only reference clones under `reference/` (paths relative to that folder). Where I could only see a source through a search-result excerpt (the SAP Fiori pages answered 403 to direct fetches), I say so. Where I could not verify something, I say that too.

## Summary of recommendations

1. Build every complex form as a wizard of 3 to 5 fixed steps, each step its own URL; fewer than 3 steps means no wizard ([USWDS step indicator](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/step-indicator/); [Carbon progress indicator](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/progress-indicator/usage/); [Atlassian progress tracker](https://atlassian.design/components/progress-tracker/usage)).
2. Show progress as "Step 2 of 4 · Items" text above a segmented bar (one segment per step, labels shown only when they fit); never a bare percentage ([USWDS](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/step-indicator/); [GOV.UK question pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/)).
3. Save the draft on every "Save and continue"; the wizard is the editing UI for a `draft`, so a user can leave at any step and resume later ([NN/g wizards, 2017](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/wizards/); [GOV.UK button text](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/button/)).
4. End every wizard with a "Check and submit" page that summarises all steps with a "Change" link per section, and return the user straight to it after a change ([GOV.UK check answers](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/); [Baymard order review](https://baymard.com/checkout-usability/benchmark/step-type/order-review)).
5. Reopening an unfinished draft lands on "Check and submit" (with incomplete sections flagged), not on step 1 ([GOV.UK task list](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/complete-multiple-tasks/)).
6. Each step is a real page reached by POST-redirect-GET so the browser Back button and the in-page Back link both work ([GOV.UK question pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/)).
7. Lists render as stacked cards on phones (title, status, amount, two metadata lines) and as a table only at widths where every column fits; never horizontal scroll ([Odoo `o_kanban_mobile`](#2-data-lists-and-line-items-on-phones-without-horizontal-scroll), code: `odoo/addons/purchase/views/purchase_views.xml:548`; [SAP Fiori responsive table pop-in](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-142/ui-elements/responsive-table/usage)).
8. Line items use the "add to a list" pattern on phones: each line is a summary card, and adding or changing a line opens a one-line form page; on wide screens the same lines show as a fitting table ([HMRC add to a list](https://design.tax.service.gov.uk/hmrc-design-patterns/add-to-a-list/); code: `odoo/addons/purchase/views/purchase_views.xml:250`, `:364`).
9. Validate format on blur, never while typing; clear an error as soon as it is fixed; validate the whole step on the server on "Save and continue" ([Baymard inline validation, 2024](https://baymard.com/blog/inline-form-validation); [NN/g error guidelines, 2019/2024](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/errors-forms-design-guidelines/)).
10. On any failed step show an error summary at the top (linked to each field, "Error:" prefix in the page title) plus the same message next to the field ([GOV.UK error summary](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/)).
11. Labels always visible above fields; no placeholder-as-label; mark only the optional fields "(optional)" ([NN/g placeholders, 2014/2018](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/form-design-placeholders/); [Carbon forms](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/forms-pattern/)).
12. Money, quantity and rate fields are `type="text"` with `inputmode="decimal"`, never `type="number"`; format with Indian grouping when the field loses focus ([GOV.UK on `type=number`, 2020](https://technology.blog.gov.uk/2020/02/24/why-the-gov-uk-design-system-team-changed-the-input-type-for-numbers/); [MDN `inputmode`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Global_attributes/inputmode)).
13. On the check page, show large amounts in words ("Ninety Four Thousand One Hundred Sixty Four") under the figure, to catch lakh/crore slips (spec §5 `InWordsINR`; [NN/g mobile input checklist, 2015](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-input-checklist/) on computed values).
14. Business dates default to today, offer "Today" and "Yesterday" shortcuts, and accept typed `dd-mm-yyyy`; a calendar may be added but never be the only way in ([GOV.UK dates](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/dates/); [MDN date input](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/input/date)).
15. Receipts: offer two buttons, "Take photo" (`capture="environment"`) and "Choose file" (no capture), each accepting images and PDF ([MDN `capture`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Attributes/capture); D-FILE-08).
16. Pick vendors, items, projects and sites with a server-searched picker (type to filter, results as tappable rows), not a long `<select>` ([NN/g dropdowns, 2017](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/drop-down-menus/); [GOV.UK accessible-autocomplete](https://alphagov.github.io/accessible-autocomplete/)).
17. Short option sets (expense type, GST category, up to about 6 choices) are radio buttons, not dropdowns ([NN/g dropdowns, 2017](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/drop-down-menus/)).
18. Every tap target is at least 44 × 44 CSS px with 8 px between targets, well above the WCAG 2.2 AA floor of 24 px ([WCAG 2.5.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html); [Apple design tips](https://developer.apple.com/design/tips); [Android touch target size](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858)).
19. One sticky bottom action bar per screen on phones holding the primary action (and at most one secondary); use `scroll-padding` so it never hides a focused field ([WCAG 2.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html); [SAP Fiori footer toolbar](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-139/ui-elements/footer-toolbar/usage)).
20. Light theme tokens: body text at least 4.5:1, input borders, icons and status badges at least 3:1, placeholders (if any) 4.5:1 ([WCAG 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html); [WCAG 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)).
21. Focus ring: a solid outline at least 2 px thick with at least 3:1 change against the unfocused state, meeting AAA 2.4.13 even though AA only needs a visible ring ([WCAG 2.4.13](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html)).
22. Document state badges always carry the state word ("Pending approval"), never colour alone ([WCAG 1.4.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)).
23. No drag-only and no swipe-only actions: line reorder uses "Move up"/"Move down" buttons; approve/reject are visible buttons ([WCAG 2.5.7](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html); [Fiori footer toolbar](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-139/ui-elements/footer-toolbar/usage)).
24. Approval inbox is a card list with a count in the navigation; approve and reject happen on the document page (not from the list), with Approve and Reject always visible in the bottom bar ([Zoho Expense approvals](https://www.zoho.com/in/expense/help/reports/approving-reports/); [Fiori footer toolbar](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-139/ui-elements/footer-toolbar/usage)).
25. Reject opens its own short page with a required reason box (1 to 1,000 characters, live counter), not a popup ([NN/g error guidelines](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/errors-forms-design-guidelines/) on modals; spec §3.2).
26. Rights matrix: never a role × DocType × right grid; choose a role, then a DocType, then set each of the 11 rights to None / Own / All in a vertical list (spec D-AUTH-10, D-AUTH-12; [WCAG 1.4.10 reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html)).
27. Confirmation after an action is a green "Success" banner above the `h1` of the next page, removed on the next navigation; errors are never toasts ([GOV.UK notification banner](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/notification-banner/); [Carbon notification](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/notification/usage/)).
28. Prevent double submits on every action button and make actions idempotent on the server too ([GOV.UK button](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/button/)).
29. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; keep motion to short fades, none needed to understand state ([WCAG 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)).
30. Sign-in allows paste and password managers (`autocomplete="username"` / `"current-password"`) and has a "Show password" toggle ([WCAG 3.3.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/accessible-authentication-minimum.html)).

**Platform touch-target guidance.** Apple: "Create controls that measure at least 44 points x 44 points" ([Apple design tips](https://developer.apple.com/design/tips); I could not load the current HIG page body to quote it). Android: targets at least 48 × 48 dp, about 9 mm, with 8 dp spacing ([Android accessibility help](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858)). WCAG 2.2 2.5.8 (AA): at least 24 × 24 CSS px, or enough spacing that 24 px circles don't overlap; inline links in sentences are exempt ([2.5.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)). 2.5.5 Target Size (Enhanced) is AAA (44 × 44).

**Where the sources disagree.** GOV.UK is cautious about progress indicators, while USWDS, Carbon and Atlassian recommend them for 3+ step linear flows. GOV.UK's caution is about indicators that list every step, allow jumping and break on small screens; its fallback is a simple "Question 3 of 9" count. The product owner requires a clear progress bar, so we combine the two: a step count with the step name (GOV.UK, USWDS) plus a compact segmented bar (USWDS), with no tappable future steps.

### Recommendation for our app

- Adopt GOV.UK's page-level patterns (Back link, Continue/"Save and continue", error summary, check answers, success banner) as the backbone, because they were built for server-rendered, progressively enhanced forms, which is exactly templ + htmx.
- Adopt USWDS/Carbon rules for the progress bar: 3 to 5 fixed steps, "Step n of N" plus step name as a heading, `aria-current="step"`, hidden status text for each segment.
- Adopt Carbon's single-column layout with top labels; allow two fields per row only for tight pairs (Qty + UoM, Rate + Tax code) and only when the row is at least 480 px wide.
- Adopt Fiori's rule that state-changing actions sit in one bottom action bar and Approve/Reject are never hidden in an overflow menu.
- Touch targets: 44 px minimum height and width for every control (48 px for inputs and the bottom-bar buttons), 8 px gaps.

## 2. Data lists and line items on phones without horizontal scroll

### Findings

**What business apps do.**
- **Odoo** builds a separate card ("kanban") template with the `o_kanban_mobile` class for small screens. The PO card shows vendor (bold), total (bold, right), then number, date and a state label in the footer (code: `odoo/addons/purchase/views/purchase_views.xml:544-569`). The expense card follows the same pattern (code: `odoo/addons/hr_expense/views/hr_expense_views.xml:394-420`). For PO **lines**, the one2many field declares `mode="list,kanban"` (line 250): an editable list on desktop and a card per line with an "Add product" control on mobile, showing product and amount on the first row, quantity + UoM on the second and unit price below (lines 364-411). Third-party Odoo 19 write-ups say kanban becomes the default on mobile when one exists, and that the web client switches on a "small" flag below about 768 px ([Cybrosys](https://www.cybrosys.com/blog/how-to-build-responsive-user-interfaces-in-odoo-19-with-owl); not official Odoo docs).
- **ERPNext/Frappe** keeps its child-table grid on phones with sticky first columns (code: `frappe/frappe/public/js/frappe/form/grid.js:1533-1580`, `frappe/frappe/public/scss/common/grid.scss:87`, `:294`), and users report the Items table "must be scrolled sideways on a phone" ([Frappe forum, v15](https://discuss.frappe.io/t/how-to-make-child-table-responsive-in-mobile-view/117493)). Its escape hatch is opening a row as a full form (`grid_row.js:326`, "open form" button). This is the pattern to **avoid** for the grid and to **keep** for the row editor.
- **Salesforce mobile** shows the first four fields of a "compact layout" in a record's highlights area and on lookup cards; compact layouts exist because space on touch screens is limited ([Salesforce compact layouts](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.218.0.salesforce1.meta/salesforce1/compact_layouts_intro.htm), older doc version).
- **SAP Fiori** responsive table: 1 to 2 columns stay, the rest "pop in" under each row as label/value pairs by priority ([responsive table](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-142/ui-elements/responsive-table/usage), search excerpt).
- **Zoho Books/Inventory mobile:** I could not find documentation of their list or receive screens on mobile; the app-store listing only says orders can be managed "with just a few taps" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1037960494)). Not used as evidence.

**Research.**
- NN/g's mobile tables article (Amy Schade, 17 Sep 2017) treats horizontal scrolling as "somewhat acceptable for large tables on mobile" only with strong cues and a locked first column, and recommends letting users filter before viewing or show/hide columns, and accordions for grouped attributes ([NN/g mobile tables](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-tables/)). Our rule bans the scrolling option, which leaves filtering, priority fields and drill-down.
- NN/g's data-table tasks (Page Laubheimer, 3 Apr 2022): find records, compare, view/edit one row, act on records. Make the first column a readable identifier; show active filters; for single-row editing prefer a separate page or non-modal panel over a modal ([NN/g data tables](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/data-tables/)).
- HMRC "add to a list": a summary page listing items added so far ("You have added 3 directors"), each with Change and Remove; a page per item; Remove asks for confirmation; "Do you need to add another?" ([HMRC add to a list](https://design.tax.service.gov.uk/hmrc-design-patterns/add-to-a-list/)). GOV.UK summary cards are for "multiple summary lists that all describe the same type of thing", with 2 to 3 card-level actions at most ([summary list](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/summary-list/)).
- WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow allows two-dimensional scrolling for "parts of the content which require two-dimensional layout … such as a table" ([1.4.10](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html)). Our rule is stricter than WCAG: no exception, so every table needs a card form.

### Recommendation for our app

**List screens (every DocType).**
1. **Card list below the "fits" width, table above it.** Each DocType declares its list fields with a priority (1 = title, 2 = right-aligned figure, 3 = status, 4+ = metadata). Phone card layout:
   - Row 1: title (PO number or "Draft 42") and amount, right-aligned, tabular figures.
   - Row 2: party or employee name.
   - Row 3: date · project/site · state badge (word + colour).
   The whole card is one link (one big target). The table version is used only when every declared column fits; if it doesn't, the layout stays as cards. No column is ever pushed off-screen.
2. **Search box at the top, filters on their own page.** On phones, "Filter" opens a filter page (or a full-height sheet); applied filters show as removable chips above the list with "Clear all", and the result count is always visible ("312 purchase orders"). NN/g's "make active filters visible" applies directly.
3. **Sort** is a single select ("Newest first", "Amount high to low") rather than tappable column headers, which don't exist in card mode.
4. **Pagination:** "Previous" / "Next" buttons plus "1–25 of 312" (spec D-NUM-21: page size 25). Avoid infinite scroll: it breaks Back and loses position on a server-rendered page.
5. **Primary "New …" button** at the top of the list (and in the bottom bar on phones if the role can create).
6. **Bulk actions** (export, later bulk approve) only on wide screens in v1.

**Line items inside documents.**
1. **Phone: add-to-a-list.** The "Items" step shows each line as a card: line number, item name (and code), "1,200.000 kg × ₹62.5000", amount on the right, tax code; card actions "Change" and "Remove". Below the list: running taxable total and "Add an item" button. "Add" or "Change" opens a one-line form page (item picker, qty + UoM, rate, tax code, optional project/site), with "Save line" returning to the list. This is Odoo's mobile line card plus Frappe's "open row as form", without Frappe's sideways grid.
2. **Desktop: the same lines as a fitting table** with keyboard entry (spec §6 asks for keyboard entry; Alpine handles row keys). Columns: #, Item, Qty, UoM, Rate, Tax, Amount, actions. That is 8 columns, which fits from roughly 900 px; below that, cards. Line-level detail that doesn't fit (description, project, site, HSN) goes into an expandable row ([Carbon expandable rows](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/)) or the line page.
3. **Reorder** with "Move up" / "Move down" buttons on the line page or card menu, not drag (WCAG 2.5.7).
4. **Many lines:** spec allows up to 500 (D-DOC-17), real POs under 100. On phones show the first 20 cards plus "Show all 64 lines", and a "Find a line" search when there are more than 20.
5. **Remove** asks "Remove line 3 (MS Angle 50x50x5)?" on its own small page or inline confirm, as HMRC does, because there is no undo.

## 3. Wizard design and how it maps onto the document lifecycle

### Findings

- **When to use a wizard.** NN/g (Raluca Budiu, 25 Jun 2017): wizards suit unfamiliar or infrequent processes; they "can quickly become annoying and overly controlling if they have to be used over and over again." Provide next/previous buttons, label steps descriptively, show the whole process with the current step highlighted, "allow users to exit the wizard midway and save state" and resume later ([NN/g wizards](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/wizards/)). This is a real tension for Priya, who raises POs daily on desktop; see the mitigations below.
- **Number of steps.** At least 3 (USWDS, Carbon, Atlassian); more than 6 means simplify (Atlassian). Fixed count: USWDS and Carbon both say not to use the indicator when the step count changes with user input.
- **Progress bar vs step indicator.** GOV.UK's fallback is a plain "Question 3 of 9" and it warns against all-steps indicators on small screens ([question pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/)); USWDS pairs a segmented bar with "Step 2 of 5" and a heading ([step indicator](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/step-indicator/)); Carbon prefers vertical layouts where space allows ([progress indicator](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/progress-indicator/usage/)).
- **Back navigation.** Back link at the top; the browser Back button must show the previous page "in the state they last saw it" ([GOV.UK question pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/)). Visited steps can be revisited; unvisited ones cannot (Atlassian; NN/g "don't let users jump ahead").
- **Review step and editing from it.** GOV.UK: a "Change" link per section with hidden text naming what changes; after the edit, Continue goes back to the check page; ask any new follow-up questions first ([check answers](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/)). Baymard: 68% of e-commerce sites send users backward through the flow to edit from the review step, which testing found "highly problematic"; place edit links next to each group ([Baymard order review](https://baymard.com/checkout-usability/benchmark/step-type/order-review)). Baymard also notes review pages get mistaken for confirmation pages, so the submit button must be unmistakable.
- **Errors per step or at the end.** Carbon: validate each step before moving on ([progress indicator](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/progress-indicator/usage/)). Server-side rules that only make sense for the whole document (approval threshold, GST place of supply) can still fail at submit; GOV.UK's error summary then links back to the field on the right step.
- **Redundant entry.** WCAG 2.2 3.3.7 (Level A): information already entered in the same process must be auto-filled or selectable ([3.3.7](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/redundant-entry.html)). Examples: the supplier's address carried into the PO; the PO's lines carried into the GRN.
- **Returning to unfinished work.** Show the task list "at the start of each returning session" and let users go back into any task ([GOV.UK task list](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/complete-multiple-tasks/)). GOV.UK One Login suggests a "Save and complete later" link under the primary action ([One Login save progress](https://sign-in.service.gov.uk/documentation/design-recommendations/save-progress)).

### Recommendation for our app

**Wizard shell (shared component).**
- Header: page title ("New purchase order"), document label ("Draft 42" once saved), then "Step 2 of 4" + step name as the `h1`/`h2`, then the segmented bar (4 segments; done = filled + check, current = filled + bold outline, to do = outlined). Step labels under the segments only when they fit (desktop); on phones only the current step's name shows. Done segments are links; future ones are not.
- Footer (sticky on phones): primary "Save and continue" on the right, "Back" as a secondary; "Save and finish later" as a text link under the bar. On the last step the primary becomes the state action ("Submit for approval", or "Submit" when no approval rule applies).
- Every step is a URL (`/po/42/edit/items`), submitted with a normal form POST and answered with a redirect (POST-redirect-GET). htmx may enhance (boosted links, partial swaps for the line list), but each step must work as a full page so Back, reload and deep links behave.

**Lifecycle mapping (spec §3.2).**

| Wizard event | Document effect |
|---|---|
| Step 1 "Save and continue" on a new document | `save` → creates the `draft` (shows "Draft 42"); audit `create` |
| Any later step "Save and continue" / "Save and finish later" | `save` on the `draft` with the `version` from the form; audit `save` |
| Reopen a draft from the list | Opens on **Check and submit** with each section marked "Complete" or "Needs attention" (task-list style), not on step 1 |
| "Change" on the check page | Opens that step; "Save and continue" returns to the check page |
| "Submit for approval" on the check page | `submit` → `pending_approval` (or `approved` if no rule); success banner on the document page |
| Pending document | Read-only summary page; "Withdraw" (R2) returns it to draft and to the wizard |
| Rejected document | Summary with the reason in a banner at the top; "Revise" → `draft` → check page |
| Cancelled → "Amend" | New draft opened on the check page, banner "Amending PO/26-27/00042" |

**Before-submit preview.** The check page shows what will happen: "This order needs approval from a Purchase Manager because it is ₹50,000 or more." (from APR `Evaluate`, read-only). This answers "why does it need approval" before the user commits.

**Conflict to flag for the lead (not decided here).** D-DOC-15 says "At least one line on every save." A wizard that saves the header on step 1 has no lines yet, so the first save would fail. Options: (a) apply the one-line rule at `submit` only and let drafts save without lines; (b) keep step 1 unsaved in the session until the first line exists. Option (a) is simpler and matches "draft saved at each step", but it departs from ERPNext, which requires the Items table on every save (the source D-DOC-15 cites), and it needs a change to the DOC test cases.

**Version conflicts between steps.** Each step carries the document `version` (D-DOC-10). If someone else saved in between, show an error summary: "Someone else changed this draft since you opened it. Your changes on this step were not saved." with a link to reload, and keep the user's typed values visible in the form so they can re-enter them.

**Keeping the wizard fast for daily desktop users (NN/g's warning).**
- Keep to 3 to 5 steps; let `Enter` submit "Save and continue"; put focus on the first field of each step.
- Default everything that can be defaulted: branch, today's date, the single allowed project/site (D-AUTH-13), payment terms and address from the party, tax code from the item, rate from the last PO for that item and vendor (later).
- The check page doubles as the "whole form at a glance" view that experienced users want.
- Don't add steps that only some documents need; a fixed step can say "Nothing needed here" instead (USWDS/Carbon: fixed step count).

**Which forms are wizards.** Wizard = more than one logical section or any line items: PO, GRN, expense claim, party (with addresses), employee, CSV import. Not a wizard (single page): sign in, change password, simple masters (UoM, tax code, expense type, project, site, payment terms), approval rule, numbering settings, reject reason, cancel/close reason.

## 4. Mobile forms

### Findings

- **Labels and placeholders.** Labels above fields; mark required/optional; no placeholder text in the field ([NN/g mobile input checklist, 14 Jun 2015](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-input-checklist/)). Placeholders disappear when typing, are often low contrast and are skipped by some screen readers; "the best solution is to have clear, visible labels that are placed outside empty form fields" ([NN/g placeholders, 11 May 2014, reviewed 2018](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/form-design-placeholders/)). Carbon: label 1 to 3 words, no colon; mark only the minority group ([forms pattern](https://carbondesignsystem.com/patterns/forms-pattern/)).
- **Keyboards.** `inputmode="decimal"` gives digits plus the locale decimal separator; `numeric` gives digits only; neither guarantees a minus key or validates anything ([MDN inputmode](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Global_attributes/inputmode)). GOV.UK moved off `type="number"` (24 Feb 2020) because of screen-reader and dictation failures, silent dropping of letters, rounding of large numbers, and values changed by scroll wheels; it recommends `type="text" inputmode="numeric"` and validating on the server ([GDS technology blog](https://technology.blog.gov.uk/2020/02/24/why-the-gov-uk-design-system-team-changed-the-input-type-for-numbers/)).
- **Defaults and device features.** Use defaults, history and computed values; use the camera where it saves typing; tolerate formats, support paste, auto-format ([NN/g mobile input checklist](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-input-checklist/)).
- **Dates.** A native `<input type="date">` displays in the browser's locale format and the author can't choose the display format; the submitted value is always `yyyy-mm-dd` ([MDN date input](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/input/date)). GOV.UK: a calendar suits dates "in the near future or recent past" and when the weekday matters, but "never make a calendar control that depends on JavaScript as the only input option"; offer relative options such as "tomorrow" ([GOV.UK dates](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/dates/)). Its date input uses three numeric boxes, never auto-tabs, and gives specific errors ("must include a month") ([date input](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/date-input/)).
- **Camera upload.** `accept="image/*" capture="environment"` asks for the rear camera; `capture` is "not Baseline" and desktops show a normal file picker ([MDN capture](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Attributes/capture)). GOV.UK file upload: error wording "The selected file must be smaller than 10MB" and "must be a JPG, PNG, PDF…"; `multiple` supported since v5.13.0 (Oct 2025) ([file upload](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/file-upload/)). Spec D-FILE-08 stores photos byte for byte, accepts HEIC, and says `capture` isn't forced.
- **Validation timing.** Validate when the user leaves a field; fixed-length fields (GSTIN, PAN, pincode) may validate once the length is reached; once shown, an error should update on each keystroke and disappear as soon as the input is right; positive confirmation reduces review effort (Edward Scott, [Baymard, 9 Jan 2024](https://baymard.com/blog/inline-form-validation)). NN/g: inline next to the field, colour plus icon, a summary only as a supplement, modals sparingly ([NN/g, 3 Feb 2019, reviewed 12 Dec 2024](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/errors-forms-design-guidelines/)).
- **Long lists.** Typing beats scrolling a long dropdown; free text needs server validation ([NN/g dropdowns, 11 Jun 2017](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/drop-down-menus/)). GOV.UK's MIT-licensed `accessible-autocomplete` enhances a server-rendered `<select>` for lists up to a few hundred options, but the team gives it only "minimal support" ([accessible-autocomplete](https://alphagov.github.io/accessible-autocomplete/)).
- **Thumb reach.** Hoober's field study (1,333 observations, 18 Feb 2013): 49% one-handed, 36% cradled, 15% two-handed, with frequent grip changes ([UXmatters 2013](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2013/02/how-do-users-really-hold-mobile-devices.php)). He later said the famous reach maps were inaccurate and that people prefer to touch the **centre**; key actions in the middle of the screen, paths to other screens along the top and bottom edges ([UXmatters, Jul 2017](https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2017/07/design-for-fingers-touch-and-people-part-3.php), via excerpt). So the "thumb zone" argument for bottom bars is weaker than often claimed; bottom bars are still justified by consistency (always in the same place, always visible) and by Fiori/Zoho practice.
- **Sticky bars and focus.** WCAG 2.2 2.4.11 (AA) fails when a sticky header/footer **entirely** hides the focused element; CSS `scroll-padding` is a sufficient technique (C43) ([2.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html)).

### Recommendation for our app

**Field types (copy these attributes into the shared field components).**

| Field | Markup | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Money (rate, amount) | `type="text" inputmode="decimal" autocomplete="off"` | Accept `₹`, spaces, Indian or international grouping (D-NUM-14). On blur, reformat as `94,164.00`. Reject more than 2 decimals with a message, never round silently (D-NUM-12). Right-aligned digits in read-only views. |
| Quantity | `type="text" inputmode="decimal"`; `inputmode="numeric"` when the UoM is whole-number (Nos, Bag) | Show the UoM as a suffix next to the field ("kg"), not inside it. Error for a fraction on a whole-number UoM names the unit. |
| Percent | `type="text" inputmode="decimal"` with "%" suffix | |
| GSTIN, PAN, pincode, HSN | `type="text"`, `autocapitalize="characters"` for GSTIN/PAN, `inputmode="numeric"` for pincode/HSN, `spellcheck="false"` | Validate when the length is complete; on a valid GSTIN, show the derived state and PAN as confirmation text under the field. |
| Email | `type="email" autocomplete="email"` (sign in: `autocomplete="username"`) | |
| Phone | `type="tel" autocomplete="tel"` | |
| Business date | See below | |
| Long text (notes, reasons) | `<textarea>` with visible character count when there is a limit | |

**Dates.** Default to today. Show "Today" and "Yesterday" as small buttons next to the field (relative dates per GOV.UK). The field itself is a text input with `inputmode="numeric"` and hint "For example, 27-03-2026", accepting the formats in D-NUM-15 and echoing the parsed date with weekday under the field ("Friday, 27 March 2026") so a day/month mix-up is visible. A native `type="date"` picker is optional: it is fine for choosing, but its display follows the phone's locale rather than our `dd-mm-yyyy`, so if used it must not be the only way to type a date. Dates far from today (a vendor bill date from last month) are rare and are typed.

**Receipts and photos.** On the expense line page, two buttons side by side: "Take photo" (`accept="image/*" capture="environment"`) and "Choose file" (`accept="image/*,application/pdf"`, no capture). This keeps D-FILE-08's un-forced picker and adds a one-tap camera path. After upload show a thumbnail (JPEG/PNG/WEBP) or a file chip (HEIC/PDF) with "Remove". Show limits before the error happens: "JPG, PNG, HEIC or PDF, up to 10 MB". Use GOV.UK's error wording.

**Pickers (party, item, project, site, employee).** A text input that searches on the server as you type (htmx, short debounce), showing up to 10 results as full-width rows: code, name, and one distinguishing line (party: city + GSTIN; item: UoM + HSN). The chosen value then shows as a summary with "Change". Without JavaScript it falls back to a search results page. Respect scope and restrictions in results (D-AUTH-13). If the list has exactly one allowed value (Suresh's sites), preselect it.

**Short choices.** Radios for up to about 6 options (expense type has 5; GST category; claim for "Myself"/"Someone else"). Radios for yes/no. Dropdowns only for medium lists that users know by heart (state).

**Validation.**
- On blur: format checks only (number shape, date shape, GSTIN check digit, length).
- On "Save and continue": full server validation of the step. Re-render with an error summary at the top ("There is a problem", each item a link to the field), the same message under the field, red border plus an icon, and "Error: " prefixed to the page `<title>`.
- Once an error is showing, clear it as the user fixes it (on input), per Baymard.
- Error wording says what is wrong and how to fix it, with the field name: "Rate must have no more than 4 decimal places".

**Layout and actions.**
- Single column on phones, max form width about 40 rem on desktop; two fields per row only for tight pairs.
- One primary button per screen. On phones the bottom bar holds the primary (full width or right) and at most one secondary; everything else (Delete draft, Print, Cancel document) goes in a "More actions" list on the page, not in the bar. Destructive actions use the warning style and a confirmation page.
- Apply `scroll-padding-bottom` equal to the bar's height so focused fields never sit under it (2.4.11). Hide the bar while the on-screen keyboard is open only if testing shows it covers the field; otherwise keep it.
- Disable the submit button after the first tap and show "Saving…"; the server rejects stale `version` anyway (D-DOC-10).

## 5. Accessibility points that matter for a light-only theme (WCAG 2.2 AA)

### Findings

- **Text contrast 1.4.3:** 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text (≥ 24 px regular or ≥ about 18.5 px bold). Placeholder text is covered. Disabled controls are exempt. Ratios are not rounded: 4.499:1 fails ([1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)).
- **Non-text contrast 1.4.11:** 3:1 for the visual information that identifies controls and their states, and for meaningful graphics. An input's border must reach 3:1 when the border is what shows there is a field (the example `#767676` on white passes); disabled controls are exempt ([1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)).
- **Focus 2.4.7 / 2.4.11 / 2.4.13:** AA needs a visible indicator (2.4.7) that is not entirely hidden by author content such as sticky bars (2.4.11). 2.4.13 Focus Appearance is **AAA**: an indicator at least as large as a 2 px perimeter of the control with 3:1 change between focused and unfocused pixels ([2.4.13](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html); [2.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html)).
- **Use of colour 1.4.1:** colour can't be the only means; F81 is "identifying required or error fields using color differences only" ([1.4.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)).
- **Reflow 1.4.10:** content works at 320 CSS px wide with vertical scrolling only, except content that needs 2-D layout ([1.4.10](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html)). Our rule removes that exception.
- **Motion 2.3.3 (AAA):** interaction-triggered motion can be turned off; `prefers-reduced-motion` is a sufficient technique ([2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)).
- **Dragging 2.5.7 (AA):** every drag action needs a single-pointer alternative, for example up/down buttons for a sortable list ([2.5.7](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html)).
- **Redundant entry 3.3.7 (A)** and **accessible authentication 3.3.8 (AA)**: see sections 3 and the sign-in screen.

### Recommendation for our app

- **Palette rules (light only).** White or near-white page background. Body text at least 4.5:1 (aim for 7:1 for primary text on white). Secondary/metadata text still at least 4.5:1: "muted grey" is the most common failure in business UIs. Input borders, checkbox and radio outlines, segmented-bar outlines, and icon-only buttons at least 3:1 against the background. Links distinguishable by underline, not colour alone.
- **Status colours.** Each state gets a badge with its word and a tint: Draft (neutral), Pending approval (amber), Approved (green), Rejected (red), Cancelled (grey with strike-free text "Cancelled"), Closed (neutral dark). Badge text on its tint must reach 4.5:1; amber and green tints usually need dark text. Negative money shows a "-" sign (D-NUM-13), never red alone.
- **Focus.** One focus style everywhere: a 2 to 3 px solid outline in a dark blue with a 2 px offset, at least 3:1 against both white and the control's own colour. This meets AAA 2.4.13 at no extra cost, which matters because a light-only theme gives no dark-mode fallback for low-visibility users.
- **Errors.** Red text plus an icon plus the word "Error" in hidden text; red border thickened to 2 px so the change isn't colour only.
- **Avoid disabled buttons as feedback.** They are exempt from contrast and so are often unreadable; prefer leaving the button active and explaining in the error summary. Where an action isn't allowed (approve your own request), hide it and say why in one line.
- **Motion:** only short fades and the htmx swap indicator; wrap them in `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- **Test widths:** 320 px (WCAG reflow), 360 px (common Android), 390 px (common iPhone), 768 px, 1280 px; and 200% zoom on desktop. The "no horizontal scroll" rule should be checked automatically in the chromedp e2e suite (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= clientWidth` on every screen at 320 px).
- **Light-only enforcement** (for the tech-research agent to confirm): declare the page as light-only so browsers and Android's automatic dark theme don't invert our colours. I did not verify the exact mechanism here.

## 6. Approvals on mobile

### Findings

- **Zoho Expense app:** approvers open "Reports Approval", open a report and tap **Approve** or **Reject** "at the bottom of the screen"; reject asks for a reason in a pop-up; approve asks to confirm. Bulk approve is described only for the web app; on mobile each report is opened and decided individually ([Zoho Expense help](https://www.zoho.com/in/expense/help/reports/approving-reports/)).
- **Salesforce:** pending requests appear in an "Items to Approve" section with Approve, Reject and Reassign; the approver can open the record and see earlier approvers' comments; notifications go only to users with record access ([Trailhead](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/it/content/learn/modules/approval-process-for-public-sector-solutions/manage-approval-requests); [Salesforce admin docs](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.salesforce1appadmin.meta/salesforce1appadmin/s1_admin_guide_notifications_approvals.htm)).
- **SAP Fiori:** Approve/Reject must stay visible on smartphones, not in overflow ([footer toolbar](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-139/ui-elements/footer-toolbar/usage), excerpt).
- **Odoo** uses a separate "refuse reason" wizard for expenses and warns approvers about possible duplicate expenses before approving (code: `odoo/addons/hr_expense/wizard/hr_expense_refuse_reason.py`, `hr_expense_approve_duplicate.py`, `odoo/addons/hr_expense/models/hr_expense.py:1428`).
- **Frappe** sends approvers action links that land on a confirmation page before applying (code: `frappe/frappe/workflow/doctype/workflow_action/workflow_action.py:133`, `:160`, `:439`). Our v1 has no email (spec §1), so in-app only.
- **Swipe actions** have no visual signifier, are moderately and unevenly known, and need a button alternative ([HAL study on iOS swipe-revealed widgets](https://hal.archives-ouvertes.fr/hal-02293836)); WCAG 2.5.1/2.5.7 also require single-pointer alternatives.
- **Modals for errors/reasons:** NN/g advises using modals sparingly ([NN/g errors](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/errors-forms-design-guidelines/)); a server-rendered page is also more robust than a popup on a phone with a keyboard open.

### Recommendation for our app

- **Inbox ("Waiting for my approval", count badge in the navigation).** Card list, oldest first by default (so nothing waits forever), each card: document type and label ("Purchase order · Draft 42"), party or employee, amount (large, right), "Submitted by Priya Shah · 2 days ago", and the reason it is with me ("₹50,000 or more"). Filter by type. No approve/reject on the card in v1: the approver must open the document. This matches Zoho's mobile flow and avoids approving unseen content.
- **Approval page.** Order matters on a phone: (1) amount and in-words, (2) who and why ("Needs your approval: Purchase Manager, ₹50,000 or more"), (3) key header facts (vendor, branch, project, required-by date), (4) lines as compact cards with totals and GST split, (5) attachments as thumbnails that open full screen, (6) approval and audit history collapsed. Expense claims show receipt thumbnails next to each expense line, since checking the receipt is the job.
- **Bottom bar:** "Approve" (primary) and "Reject" (secondary, not red: rejection is reversible via revise) always visible. Approve applies immediately (it is a normal, expected action), then shows the next page with a success banner "Approved. PO/26-27/00001 has been numbered." and a "Next request" link. If self-approval is blocked, the bar shows a one-line explanation instead of the buttons.
- **Reject page:** its own URL with the document summary line, a required "Reason for rejecting" textarea (1 to 1,000 characters, live counter, spec §3.2), and "Reject and send back". The reason shows to the submitter at the top of their rejected document.
- **"My requests"** for submitters: the same card list filtered to my documents with state badges; pending ones offer "Withdraw" (R2) on the document page.
- **Stale approvals:** if the document changed or was withdrawn while open, the approve POST fails the version check and shows "This request was withdrawn by Priya at 11:02. Nothing was approved."

3. **Receipt required for expenses:** the check page can flag missing receipts; whether it blocks submit is an Expenses-module policy.
4. **Repeat-use speed for purchase staff:** if Priya finds the PO wizard slow in testing, the next step is more defaults and a "Duplicate this PO" action, not dropping the wizard.
