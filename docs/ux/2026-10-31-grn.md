## 1. Goal

Today a storekeeper signs the vendor's challan at the gate and the paper reaches the office days later; nobody knows what is still to come on an order. After this ships:

- Suresh, at the gate on a phone in gloves, picks the order, checks the quantities (already filled with what is pending), photographs the challan and submits. The receipt is numbered at once (no approval rule is seeded) and the PO's pending quantity drops.
- Goods rejected at the gate are recorded with a reason and stay pending on the PO.
- Bill details (invoice no., date, total) are added later, on the approved receipt, from the invoice photo, by anyone with `write`; office staff key their accounting system from the `Bills to enter` report.
- Buyers see on the PO which receipts came and what is still pending.

We will know it works when:

- **A five-line GRN on a 360 px phone takes under a minute** (spec §11): from tapping `New goods receipt` on the Goods Receipts list to the success banner `Approved. GR/26-27/00002 has been numbered.`, with all five lines received in full, the challan number typed, one photo taken, camera and upload time excluded (as the attachments design measures it), on the e2e harness. The three menu taps before `New goods receipt` are not counted. Budget: **9 taps, about 43 s** (§5.4). `TestE2E_GRNFlow` (GRN-036) measures and reports the time; it doesn't assert it (plan S6), because harness timings aren't a phone on mobile data.
- Every quantity input and button Suresh needs is at least 44 × 44 px with 8 px gaps, and the action bar's buttons 48 px (sweep target check, GUX-030).
- In the first month, no receipt is entered against the wrong order without the PO number and vendor on screen at the moment of choosing (design check: §5.2), and no "what is still pending on this order?" call is needed (owner check, not a test).

## 3. Jobs and flow

### 3.1 Jobs

- When a truck arrives, I want to pick its order by vendor or PO number and see what is still pending, so I check the delivery against the order and not from memory.
- When everything arrived, I want the quantities already filled in, so I only confirm them.
- When part of a delivery is damaged or wrong, I want to record how much I rejected and why, so the vendor knows and the rest stays pending.
- When I have the challan in my hand, I want to type its number and photograph it, so the office has it without the paper.
- When the bill arrives days later, I want to add its photo and details to the approved receipt, so accounts can key it without anyone cancelling anything.
- When a receipt was wrong, I (a manager) want to cancel it with a reason, and Stores wants to correct it as an amendment, so pending on the PO is right again.
- When I key bills into our accounting system, I want a list of receipts with and without bill details, so nothing is keyed twice or missed.
- When I review a vendor, I want to see what was rejected and why, so I can raise it with them.
- When the driver says the vendor won't send the rest, I want to say so on the receipt, so the buyer can close the order instead of chasing it.
- When goods turn out bad after I accepted them, I want a clear way to send them back and correct the receipt, so the order's pending and the bill stay right.

### 3.2 Main flow: receive a delivery (GRN-036)

1. **Suresh** opens the menu and taps `Goods Receipts` (Documents group, `routes.go:406`; shown to everyone with `read`). The list shows his sites' receipts and `New goods receipt` (docscreen's label, `list.go:68`). He taps it.
2. **Step 1 of 4, Choose purchase order.** The page lists the approved POs with something pending at his sites as radio rows: `PO/26-27/00001 · Shree Steel Traders`, hint `Metro Depot Site` and `2 of 2 lines pending · ordered 24-10-2026`. If only one PO is visible, it is already selected. `Receipt date` shows today. He taps the row the challan names, then `Save and continue`. The draft is created with one line per PO line with pending, each received quantity set to its pending (spec §11), and `Draft 57` appears in the title line.
3. **Step 2 of 4, Delivery details.** Under `Document with the goods` he taps `Delivery challan` (or `Tax invoice` when the vendor sent the invoice with the truck), types its number under `Challan or invoice no.`, and optionally the challan date, vehicle no., transporter and e-way bill no. `Save and continue`.
4. **Step 3 of 4, Received quantities.** One card per PO line: `Ordered 1,200 · Pending 400 KGS`, `Received now` = 400. Ten bars are bent: he opens `Some rejected`, types `10` and `Bent pieces`. `Save and continue`.
5. **Step 4 of 4, Check and submit.** The note `Goods for Metro Depot Site, from Shree Steel Traders on PO/26-27/00001.` *(PROPOSED)*, the three sections, the Attachments card: he taps `Take photo`, photographs the challan, and the page reloads with the thumbnail. The accepted value `₹29,175.00` and its words. He taps `Submit`.
6. The document page shows `Approved. GR/26-27/00002 has been numbered.` (`page.go:306`). The PO now reads `Part received` and line 1 has `Pending 10 KGS` (the rejected 10 KGS stays pending, CD-234).

### 3.3 Other flows

- **From the PO page** *(PROPOSED, Q5)*: on an approved PO, a Stores user's action bar shows `Receive goods`, which opens step 1 with that PO selected (`/doc/goodsreceipt.Receipt/new?po=1`).
- **Another truck on a fully received order.** The PO isn't in the default list. Suresh taps `Show fully received orders`, picks it, and continues; step 3's cards show `Pending 0` with `Received now` blank; whatever he types warns as over-delivery (CD-234, CD-238) and submits.
- **Only some lines arrived.** On step 3 he taps `Clear all`, then types quantities on the two lines that came. Lines left blank are not stored (spec §3.2).
- **Tax invoice at the gate** (CD-272 Gap 5). Suresh chose `Tax invoice` on step 2. On the approved receipt the Bill details form is already filled with that number and date and the note `Copied from the tax invoice entered at the gate. Check and save.`; Suresh or the office checks it and taps `Save bill details`. Nothing is stored until then.
- **Vendor says the rest won't come** (CD-272 Gap 8). The driver says the vendor can't supply the balance. On step 2 Suresh ticks `Vendor says the rest won't come`. On approval the PO gets the comment `Vendor says the rest won't come. Close this order if nothing more is due.` (purchase's text, CD-276) and appears in the buyers' `To close` queue on the Purchase home. The PO's state doesn't change; Priya decides whether to close it. Cancelling the receipt takes the PO out of `To close` (the comment stays).
- **Late bill.** Days later the invoice arrives. Suresh (or the office) opens the approved receipt. The action bar's `Add bill details` (G5) jumps to the Bill details card. He adds the invoice photo under Attachments first, then types `Vendor invoice no.`, `Invoice date` and, optionally, `Invoice total`, and taps `Save bill details` *(PROPOSED)*.
- **Return after acceptance: cancel and amend (the v1 return path, CD-272 Gap 1; GRN-042).** There is no return document in v1. A week after `GR/26-27/00001` accepted 800 KGS, 100 KGS turn out bad and go back to Shree.
  1. Suresh tells Ravi (Stores has no cancel, spec §10).
  2. **Ravi** opens the receipt, More actions, `Cancel`. The cancel page shows `This receipt has bill details (SST/2627/0457). Correct your accounts too.`, and if the PO has been closed meanwhile also `PO/26-27/00001 is closed. After this cancel, only an amendment of this receipt can receive against it again.` He types the reason (`100 KGS returned to vendor: cracked`) and taps `Cancel goods receipt`. Banner `Cancelled.`; the PO's pending goes back up (400 → 1,200 KGS in the test's walk).
  3. **Suresh** opens the cancelled receipt; the bar shows `Amend`. The new draft opens on Check and submit with `Amending GR/26-27/00001`. He taps `Change` on Received quantities, sets line 1 to 700, `Save and continue`, `Submit`.
  4. `Approved. GR/26-27/00001-1 has been numbered.` The PO's pending reads 500 KGS. The amendment's Bill details show the original's invoice, copied on approval (the card reads `Copied from GR/26-27/00001`); the cancelled receipt keeps its own, read-only.
  5. If the period lock covers the receipt's date, step 2 is refused on the cancel page with the lock text; the fix is in the accounting system, or an Administrator moves the lock (spec §5).
  This works against a closed PO too, because the amendment replaces a cancelled receipt of that PO (CD-272 Gap 2, plan G0c); a fresh receipt against the closed PO is still refused with DOC-017's text.
- **Wrong receipt** (a typing mistake found later): the same cancel and amend.
- **Wrong order picked.** Once the draft has lines its PO is fixed (spec §3.1). Step 1 shows the PO as text with `Delete this draft to receive against another order.` and a `Delete draft` link for its creator.
- **Service order.** For a PO with only service lines (crane hire), step 2 hides the document kind, number and transport fields and keeps `Remarks` and the tick; step 3 shows `Ordered 8 · Pending 8 HRS`.
- **Bills.** Priya opens `Goods receipt reports` *(PROPOSED nav entry, gap R2)*, then `Bills to enter`, taps the chip `No bill details yet` and exports CSV.

### 3.5 Who sees what on the GRN document page

"Bar" is the action bar's primary; brackets are More actions. GRNs have no Close (`CloseRight: "none"`, spec §14 item 4) and no pending state unless an owner adds an approval rule (then docscreen's generic pending page applies, unchanged).

**Not the purchase owner rule.** Stores holds `write` with scope `all` (spec §10), so any storekeeper may finish another's draft. There is no owner banner, no hand-over and no comments card on a GRN (none is in spec v0.5).

**Cards**

- **One card per PO line, including fully received ones** (spec §11), in PO line order. Card title `{n} · {item name}` where `n` is the **PO line number** (AD-12 decision, plan note P2), then the item code (`snap_item_code` from the PO line), then the input with its hint.
- **`Received now`** is prefilled with the stored received quantity, which step 1 set to pending (spec §11). A line with pending 0 has no stored line, so its input is blank. Whole-number units (Bag, Nos, Box) use `inputmode="numeric"`; the rest `decimal` (`QtyInput`, `inputs.templ:118`). The unit is the PO line's (spec §3.2), as the suffix.
- **Hint `Ordered 1,200 · Pending 400 KGS`** (spec §11 wording, quantities through `FormatQtyUoM`) is drawn above the input, as `field.templ` always draws a hint, and is read with it (`aria-describedby`). Pending is the PO's pending before this receipt: `max(0, qty − already_received − accepted on other approved and closed GRNs)` (spec §4).
- **Fully received lines** (pending 0): spec §11 lists them, so another truck can still be entered. When the PO still has pending lines, they are grouped after the pending ones in a closed `Disclosure` `Fully received lines ({n})` *(PROPOSED, Q3)*, keeping the five lines Suresh actually counts on one screen. When no line is pending (a truck against a fully received PO), every card shows open in line order. Inputs inside a closed disclosure still post. The disclosure opens when any of its inputs has a value or an error.
- **`Some rejected`** *(spec wording)*: a `Disclosure` per card, closed by default, open when rejected is above 0 or has an error. Inside: `Rejected` (`QtyInput`, same unit) and `Reason for rejecting` (`TextInput`, max 200). After a save with rejects, the card shows `Accepted {x} {uom}` *(PROPOSED)* under them; accepted is computed, never typed (spec §3.2).
- **Over-delivery warning** (spec §4, CD-234): when a card's accepted quantity is above its pending, the card shows `components.Warnings` with `Line {n}: Accepted {x} is more than the {y} pending on {PO number}.`, e.g. `Line 1: Accepted 450 KGS is more than the 400 KGS pending on PO/26-27/00001.` It compares accepted, so 450 received with 50 rejected doesn't warn (GRN-013). It shows on every render of the step from stored or posted values (after `Receive all pending`, `Clear all`, Back from the check page, a 422). There is no live check while typing (no request per keystroke on a weak network; CLAUDE.md: a fragment never redraws the input being typed in). The check page lists every such warning before Submit (§5.5).
- **Hard stop against typing mistakes** (CD-272 Gap 3): on save, an accepted quantity above twice the PO line's ordered quantity is refused (422, nothing written) with `Line {n}: Accepted {x} is more than twice the {y} ordered. Check the quantity.`, e.g. `Line 1: Accepted 2,400.01 KGS is more than twice the 1,200 KGS ordered. Check the quantity.` (GRN-043), under that card's `Received now` and linked from the summary (R1). Exactly twice saves with the over-delivery warning; received above twice with enough rejected to bring accepted under it saves. The card's hint shows the ordered quantity, so the cause is on screen.
- **Accessible name.** Each `Received now` input carries `aria-label="Received now, line {n}, {item name}"` through `FieldProps.Attrs` (visible label first, WCAG 2.5.3), so a screen reader knows which of 30 identical labels it is in. Same for `Rejected` and `Reason for rejecting`.

**`Receive all pending` and `Clear all`** (spec §11)

- Both are secondary submit buttons in the step form with `name="nav" value="stay"` and a `formaction` adding `?fill=all` or `?fill=clear` *(PROPOSED query)*. htmx 2.0.11 honours the clicked button's `formaction` (`htmx.min.js`, `lastButtonClicked`), and browsers do without JavaScript. - **Unit conversion at the gate**, **quality inspection**, **one bill across several receipts**: a unit choice per card; a second quantity or a step within the five-step limit; the duplicate-invoice warning becoming a link or bill details becoming their own record.
