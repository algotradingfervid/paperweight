## 1. Goal

Today Deepak keeps paper bills in an envelope, fills a spreadsheet after the trip, and hands both to his manager; advances are tracked in someone's notebook. After this ships:

- Deepak adds each expense on his phone when he spends it: photo first, then type, date and amount. The claim adds up, works out the period, sets off his advance and shows what he will be paid.
- He asks for an advance before a trip on the same kind of screen, and sees what is left of it on every later claim.
- Priya approves on her phone with each receipt next to its line and opens any receipt full screen with one tap. She rejects with a reason; she never edits his figures (CD-234).
- Meena or Anil closes an approved claim or advance with a note of how it was settled.
- The office keys its accounting system from the Expense lines CSV, month by month.

We will know it works when:

- **One expense with a photo takes under 30 seconds on a 360 px phone**, from tapping `Add an expense` on the Expenses step to being back on the Expenses step with the new card (spec §12). Camera time is included; the photo uploads while Deepak picks the type and types the amount. Seven taps, one scroll and the digits of the amount at 360 × 640 (§5.5, tap table), assuming an upload speed of at least 5 Mbit/s (good 4G); on a slower link `Save line` waits for the upload (E5). `TestE2E_ExpenseFlow` (EXP-038, EUX-001) checks the tap and scroll count with a stub camera file; the 30 seconds is a human check on a real phone on mobile data before go-live.
- Priya decides a 10-line claim on a 390 px phone in under 60 seconds, opening at least one receipt full screen and coming back (owner check on a real phone).
- No claim is returned in the first month because a receipt was missing without a reason: the check page names every such line before submit (§5.6).

## 3. Jobs and flow

### 3.1 Jobs

- When I've just paid a bill, I want to photograph it and type the amount in a few seconds, so I don't lose the bill or forget what it was for.
- When I'm going on a site trip, I want to ask for an advance, so I don't spend my own money first.
- When I submit a claim, I want the advance I took set against it and to see what I'll be paid, so I don't have to work it out.
- When a bill has no receipt (a rickshaw, a tea stall), I want to say why, so my claim isn't sent back.
- When one hotel bill covers two nights, I want to use the same photo on both lines, so I don't photograph it twice.
- When I approve a claim, I want each receipt beside its line and to open it full screen, so I can check the figure without asking.
- When something is wrong on one line, I want to send the claim back with a reason, so the claimant fixes it (I can't change it).
- When a claim or advance is settled, I (Director or Administrator) want to close it with a note of how, so nobody wonders whether it was paid.
- When I key the month's expenses into the accounting system, I want every line with its GST details in one file.

### 3.2 Main flow: a claim with a photo, set against an advance (EXP-038)

1. **Deepak** opens the menu, then `Expense Claims` (nav entry from the DocType's plural label, `internal/app/routes.go:390-404`; there is no separate "Expenses" entry). The list shows his claims. He taps `New expense claim` (docscreen's label, `list.go:68`).
2. **Claim details** (step 1 of 3): he types `Pune Metro site visit` in `Purpose`. `Claim date` is today. He opens `Set against an advance`, which lists his one open advance as `EA/26-27/00003 · ₹4,000.00 left`, and taps it. He leaves `Less advance` blank: the claim then sets off as much as the advance allows, worked out on every save and written at submit (CD-264 (6)). `Save and continue` creates `Draft 42`.
3. **Expenses** (step 2 of 3) shows `Add an expense` directly under the heading (§6 E2). He taps it.
4. **Expense line page:** he taps `Take photo`, photographs the bill and confirms. While `Uploading…` shows under the buttons, he taps `Food`, scrolls once, taps `Amount`, types `450`, keeps today's date and taps `Save line`. He is back on Expenses with card 1: thumbnail, `Food`, `24-10-2026`, `₹450.00`. (Under 30 seconds, §5.5.)
5. He repeats step 4 for each bill during the trip. For the second night of the same hotel bill he opens `Same receipt as another expense` on the line page and picks `Same receipt as line 2`.
6. A rickshaw fare has no bill: on its line page he opens `No receipt` and types `Auto rickshaw, no bill given`.
7. **Check and submit** (step 3 of 3) shows each expense with its thumbnail, the total `₹6,450.00` and its words, `Less advance (EA/26-27/00003) ₹4,000.00`, `To pay ₹2,450.00`, any duplicate warnings, and the approval preview `This expense claim needs approval from Priya Shah.` He taps `Submit for approval`.
8. The claim page shows `Submitted for approval to Priya Shah or Fallback Approver.` (docscreen `page.go:280` with APR's `inbox.go:198-200`) and `Waiting for approval from Priya Shah or Fallback Approver`.
9. **Priya** taps the inbox icon, opens the claim, reads `Needs your approval: Deepak Nair's reporting manager, Any amount` (§5.8, CD-266), scrolls the lines, taps a thumbnail to see the bill full screen, comes back, and taps `Approve`. `Approved. EX/26-27/00004 has been numbered.` and `Next request`.
10. **Meena** later opens `EX/26-27/00004`, More actions, `Close`, types `Paid by NEFT 30-10-2026` under the hint `For example how it was paid`, and taps `Close expense claim`.

### 3.3 Other flows

- **Advance.** Before the trip Deepak opens `Employee Advances`, `New employee advance`, types the purpose and date, adds one item (`Travel`, `₹10,000.00`, note `Bus and local travel`), and submits. Priya approves. Meena pays it outside the app (CD-234). Claims then list it under `Set against an advance` while something is left.
- **Advance settled.** When nothing more will be claimed, Meena opens the advance (`₹10,000.00`, `Used ₹6,000.00`, `Left ₹4,000.00`), More actions, `Close`. The page says what is left and asks how it was settled; she types `₹4,000 returned by NEFT 1234 on 30-11-2026`.
- **Rejected and revised.** Priya taps `Reject`, types `Line 3: the fuel bill is for 29-10, after the trip. Please check.` and taps `Reject and send back`. Deepak sees the reason in a danger banner at the top of the claim, taps `Revise`, lands on the check page, fixes line 3 and submits again.
- **Withdraw.** Deepak, while the claim is pending, taps `Withdraw`; the claim is a draft again and opens on its check page.
- **Cancel and amend.** Meena cancels an approved claim with a reason. Deepak taps `Amend` on the cancelled claim: a new draft opens on its check page with `Amending EX/26-27/00004`, every line and receipt copied (FILE-043, EXP-008).
- **Back-entry.** Anil opens `New expense claim`, types `EMP-008` in `Employee code (optional)`, and enters a leaver's paper claim with real dates and `Paper file` as each line's reason (spec §14).

## 5. Screens

**Common to every wizard step** (design-system §8.3, docscreen as built): title line, `Stepper` (`Step 2 of 3` and the step name as `h1`), conflict banner and `ErrorSummary` when present, the step form, the action bar (`Save and continue` primary, `Back` secondary, not on step 1), and `Save and finish later` under the bar. From 80rem the state badge and Approval history move to the side panel. No autofocus.

**Fields hidden on every screen by `Module.Hide`** (presentation only; `Validate` enforces the rules, hooks spec §4.3): the snapshots `snap_employee_name`, `snap_employee_code`, `snap_type_code`, `snap_type_name`, `snap_project_code`, `snap_site_code`; `receipt_sha256` (the line page posts it itself, §5.5); `period_from` and `period_to`, which both carry the label `Period` and would show as two `Period` rows, so the page shows one pair `Period  20-10-2026 to 24-10-2026` *(PROPOSED format)* through `PageExtra.Pairs`; `employee_id` for everyone except an Administrator (spec §3.1). - **Open advances:** the employee's approved advances with something left (amount − used, counting approved and closed claims, spec §4), read through `Auth.Scope` (hooks spec §5 rule 4), oldest first. On `/new` (`View` nil) the employee is the creator's (`auth.Directory.EmployeeForUser`); for an Administrator who typed a code, the radios appear after the first save, because the employee is known only then.
- **Administrator back-entry with an advance:** `Save and continue` moves Anil to Expenses, so he goes `Back` to Claim details to pick the advance (now listed), then on. A G6 `nav=stay` bar isn't used: the bar is drawn before the code is typed, so it can't know whether that employee has an open advance, and a Stay button on every Administrator claim would add a click to the claims with none. Back-entry is rare and on a desktop.
- **No open advance:** the disclosure isn't shown at all (fewer choices, §4.12). An Administrator entering for someone else sees it once the draft exists (above).
- **Disclosure open** when an advance is chosen, or `Less advance` has a value or an error; closed otherwise.
- **A chosen advance that has since been closed or cancelled:** it stays in the list, checked, with the hint `This advance is closed. Choose another, or No advance.` *(PROPOSED)* (or `cancelled`). Submit is refused with DOC-017's text (spec §4).
- **Six or more advances:** still radios, one per advance (a person rarely has more than two open).
- **Less advance blank** (CD-264 (6), spec §3.1): stored as blank, meaning as much as the advance allows: the smaller of what's left and the claim total, worked out on every save (so the Expenses totals and the check page show the figure as lines are added) and written to `less_advance` at submit. The input stays blank on reopening; the hint states the rule. A typed figure is kept as typed. `Less advance can't be more than the total.` is a submit check (spec §5 as amended), because the advance is picked before any line exists: a typed figure above the total saves as a draft and the failed submit returns to this step with the message at `Less advance`.

## 7. Checklist

- [x] **One job and one primary button per phone screen (§4.1).** Each screen in §5 names one primary: `Save and continue`, `Save line`, `Submit for approval`, `Approve`, the reason pages' button. Upload buttons and `Remove` are secondary; `Reject` is the bar's secondary. The most frequent action sits nearest the thumb: `Add an expense` directly under the Expenses heading (E2), `Type` and `Amount` first in the line form, `Save line` in the sticky bar (E4); one scroll remains per expense (§5.5 tap table).
- [x] **Renders fully without JavaScript (§4.2).** Every page is a GET URL with normal form posts; disclosures are `<details>`; the advance and type choices are radios. Exception, as in the attachments design: the upload buttons need htmx (catalogue "htmx + app.js", CD-133(c)); without them a line is saved with a reason or a same-receipt choice (§5.5).
- [x] **Business words (§4.3, §9).** `Draft 42`, `EX/26-27/00004`, `EA/26-27/00003`, `₹6,450.00`, `dd-mm-yyyy`, IST times; type names, not codes; `No receipt`, `Left`, `To pay` as words.
- [x] **Nothing wider than 320 px (§1.3, §4.4).** One column; cards wrap long notes, reasons and purposes (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` in the components); the 64 px thumbnail sits beside text that wraps; upload buttons stack below about 370 px (CD-251); reports keep 8 DataList columns and put the rest in the CSV. Sweep variants in §6.1, EUX-027.
- [x] **Targets (§4.5).** Radio rows, disclosure summaries, `Today`/`Yesterday`, upload buttons and the thumbnail link are at least 44 px; the bar's buttons 48 px. E3 sets the thumbnail at 64 px.
- [x] **Never colour alone (§4.6).** State badges carry words and icons; `No receipt yet` has an icon and words; warnings are text in a banner with an icon.
- [x] **Defaults (§4.7).** Claim date and line date today; the employee from the user; less advance from what's left and the total; tax code and HSN from the type; the advance list filtered to this employee's open ones; `Submit for approval` labelled from the preview.
- [x] **Wizards (§8.3).** Claim: Claim details → Expenses → Check and submit (3 fixed steps). Advance: Details → Items → Check and submit. Line pages, reason pages and reports are single pages.
- [x] **Labels, hints, errors.** Every input has a visible label and "(optional)" where optional; hints come before the input; every §5 message has a place (§5.17), next to its field and in the summary.
- [x] **Destructive actions confirm (§8.5).** Delete draft, Remove line and Remove file keep their confirms; Cancel and Close need a reason; Approve doesn't confirm.
- [x] **Nothing typed is lost on Back, 422 or 409.** Wizard Back saves without validating; custom bodies redraw from the post (`StepState.Posted`), and `Line.Top` redraws the posted receipt; the upload answer replaces only `#receipt-files` and the hidden hash; a 409 and a 503 keep typed values (HOOK-006, EUX-034).
- [x] **Visibility of system status (Nielsen 1).** `Uploading…` with a progress bar; the thumbnail appears when the upload is done; `No receipt yet` on the card; the preview names the approver; the submit banner names who it went to; `Needs your approval: Deepak Nair's reporting manager, Any amount` for the approver (CD-266).
- [x] **Recognition over recall (Nielsen 6).** Advances show what's left, purpose, date and amount; same-receipt options show type, date and amount; the approver sees each bill beside its line.
- [x] **WCAG 2.2 AA.** Token colours only; radios in fieldsets with legends; thumbnails have alt text naming the line; `ErrorSummary` focused after a 422; upload status `role="status"`; refusals in `#alerts` (`role="alert"`); the sticky bar's scroll padding keeps focus visible. axe zero violations (EUX-029).
- [x] **Speed (§4.8).** Server-rendered pages; the photo uploads while the user types; thumbnails load lazily (E8 notes the size); busy labels on every submit.
- [x] **The system works things out (§4.9, §4.12).** Total, to pay, period, the default less advance, type defaults for GST fields, the approver, duplicate checks and amounts in words are computed.
- [x] **Next step's choices only (§4.12).** The advance, same-receipt, no-receipt, GST and project disclosures are closed unless they hold something; the advance disclosure is absent with no open advance; Close and Cancel are under More actions.
- [x] **Consistent (§4.11).** Generic docscreen patterns, the catalogue's FileUpload with the logo page's swap behaviour, the attachments design's texts and focus rules, docscreen's reason pages; the reports follow the purchase pending-items report; how the reports are reached is the one place expense can't copy purchase, which has a home page (Q5).
