## 1. Goal

Today a site person phones or WhatsApps the buyer, the buyer types a PO, and anyone with the right can edit anyone's draft. After this ships:

- Suresh raises a purchase request on his phone and later sees `Ordered` and the PO number on it, without asking anyone.
- Priya turns open request lines into a PO in a few taps, with the last price filled in, and records that the PO went to the vendor.
- Ravi and Meena approve from the phone, seeing the last price paid next to each line.
- Nobody but a draft's owner can change it. Everyone else sees who owns it, comments on it, or (a manager) hands it over.
- Each person opens Purchase and sees only the work waiting for them.

We will know it works when:

- Suresh, on a 360 px phone, raises a two-line request in **under 90 seconds** from tapping `New request` to the success banner (timed in `TestE2E_RequestToSentFlow`, PO-068).
- Priya creates a PO from a request (vendor picked, last price filled) and reaches Check and submit in **under 60 seconds** on desktop.
- In the first month, no draft is changed by anyone but its owner (guaranteed by rights; PO-058), and no "who is doing this PO?" call is needed (owner check, not a test).
- Every count on the Purchase home matches its queue page (PO-070).

## 3. Jobs and flow

### 3.1 Jobs

- When I need material at the site, I want to raise a request from my phone in a minute, so the buyer has exactly what I need without a phone call.
- When I've raised a request, I want to see whether it's ordered, on which PO and with whom, so I know when to expect it and whom to ask.
- When requests are waiting, I want to pull their lines into one PO for a vendor, so I don't retype items or order the same thing twice.
- When I add a line, I want the last price paid filled in, so I don't search old POs.
- When a draft isn't mine, I want to see whose it is and leave a comment, so I never overwrite a colleague's work.
- When a colleague is away, I (a manager) want to hand their draft to someone else, so the order isn't stuck.
- When a PO is approved, I want to get it to the vendor and record that I did, so nobody wonders whether it went.
- When I approve, I want to see what we paid last time for each line, so I can question a price without leaving the page.
- When part of an order won't come, I want to close it with a reason, so Stores stops expecting it and the rest can be ordered again.
- When I open Purchase, I want only the work waiting for me, so I don't hunt through lists.

### 3.2 Main flow: request to sent order (PO-068)

1. **Suresh** taps the menu, then `Purchase`. The Purchase home shows `My requests` and the action bar `New request` (he has no `create` on Purchase Order, so `New purchase order` isn't there).
2. `New request` opens the request wizard at Details. `Needed at` offers his two sites; he picks METRO-SITE. Project is filled from the site on save. He sets `Needed by` with the date input and taps `Save and continue`. `Draft 61` is created.
3. On Items he taps `Add an item`, picks `CEMENT-OPC`, types `40`, and the unit defaults to Bag. `Save line`. He adds `WIRE-18G`, 25 kg, the same way.
4. Check and submit shows both lines, the Attachments card (he can `Take photo` of a sample, Q9) and the Comments card. He taps `Submit request` (Q5). The request is numbered at once (no approval) and the page shows the success banner, `Submitted` and `Not ordered`.
5. **Priya** opens Purchase. `Requests to order` shows `1 request`. She opens the queue, then `PR/26-27/00001`, and taps `Create PO`.
6. The Create PO page lists the two pending rows and asks for the vendor. She picks `SHREE` and taps `Create purchase order` *(PROPOSED)*. A draft PO is created with METRO-SITE, PRJ-METRO, the needed-by date and both rows at the last price paid to Shree for this project. It opens at Details with a success banner.
7. She checks Terms (Shree's primary billing address is selected), Items (each card shows `Last bought at …`), and Check and submit (place reason, approval preview `This purchase order needs approval from a Purchase Manager because it is ₹50,000 or more.`). `Submit for approval`.
8. **Ravi** opens the approval inbox on his phone, opens the PO, reads the lines with their last prices and the comments, and taps `Approve`.
9. **Priya** sees the PO in `To send`. She opens it. The action bar shows `Send to vendor` (G5). The Send page lets her get the PO as a file (Q1), then record `Sent on` today, `How`: WhatsApp, and `Mark as sent`. The PO page's Sent card now reads `Sent to vendor on 31-10-2026 by WhatsApp (Priya Shah)`.
10. **Suresh** opens his request: `Ordered`, and the Linked orders card lists `PO/26-27/00042 · Shree Steel Traders · Approved · Not received`.

### 3.3 Other flows

- **Add from requests (inside a PO).** On Items, `Add from requests (2 open)` opens the list of open request lines for this PO's site and project. Priya ticks lines, adjusts quantities, taps `Add selected`, and is back on Items with the lines appended.
- **Copy.** On any PO she can read, Priya chooses `Copy` under More actions. A new draft owned by her opens on Details; the check page lists what was copied and what was left out.
- **Hand over.** Ravi opens Priya's draft (read-only for him), taps `Hand over` in the owner banner, picks Arjun, adds a note, `Hand over`. Arjun now owns it; the note is the first new comment; Priya sees the banner `Owned by Arjun Mehta. …` next time she opens it.
- **Comment instead of edit.** Arjun opens Priya's draft, reads the banner, types a comment, `Post comment`. Priya sees it on her check page and on the Purchase home under `Recent comments`.
- **Close short.** 25 of 40 bags arrived and the vendor can't supply the rest. Priya opens the PO, More actions, `Close`. The close page warns `1 line still has 15 Bag pending. …` (Q12), she types a reason, `Close purchase order`. Suresh's request now shows 15 Bag pending to order again (§20.2).
- **Request no longer needed.** Suresh opens his request, More actions, `Close`. If a draft PO still holds a row, the page warns `{n} rows are on orders not yet approved: …`. He closes it; the buyer's PO can't be approved until the row is removed.

### 3.4 Alternative and failure paths

| Case | What the user sees |
|---|---|
| No right to the module (no `read` on Order or Request) | No `Purchase` nav entry. A typed URL gets AUTH's 403 text. |
| No right to a document, or the document is hidden by a restriction | AUTH-108's 404 `This record doesn't exist or you don't have access to it.`, the same as a missing one (CD-245). This also applies to comment posts (§22). |
| Not the owner of a draft or rejected PO | The read-only document page with the owner banner (G1, G3). There is no Change link, no `Add an item`, no Submit, and the step URLs send them to the document page. |
| Owner changed while I was editing (hand-over mid-edit, review focus #2) | The next save is a 409 with DOC-069's text, for example `Someone else changed Draft 57 after you opened it. Reload it and make your changes again.`, with the typed values kept. Reload opens the read-only page (G1) with `Owned by Arjun Mehta. …`. |
| Two buyers tick the same request line (review focus #1) | The second `Add selected` or save is a 422: `Line {n}: Request {PR} line {m} is already on {PO display}.` (spec §20.5), naming the first PO. Nothing is written, and the ticks and quantities are kept. |
| Validation errors (422) | `ErrorSummary` at the top, focused, with each message next to its field. The texts are the spec's (§4, §20.2, §20.5, §21.2, §22, §23) or the kernel's. |
| Stale page (409) on any write | DOC-069's text, with the document's display, in the conflict banner and a reload link. Typed values stay. |
| The record changed state underneath (approved in another tab, withdrawn, already decided) | The APR or DOC message, unchanged, for example `PO/26-27/00001 is no longer waiting for approval. It was approved by Ravi Kulkarni.` |
| A request row became closed or cancelled under a draft PO | On the PO's next save or approve: DOC's text with its `Line {n}: ` prefix, for example `Line 2: PR/26-27/00001 is closed. Remove the line or pick another purchase request.` (or `cancelled`), next to the row. Spec §20.5 quotes only the tail; the prefix is DOC's (CD-253, CD-142). |
| Server busy (503) | `The system is busy. Please try again in a moment.` (`db.ErrBusy`), in the page's alerts region. Nothing is lost. |
| Nothing to show | Each list, queue, card and report has its own empty text (§5). The home says `Nothing waiting for you.` |
